I finally moved this blog from Gatsby to Astro.

Gatsby served me well for years. It helped me move away from older setups, and at that time it was exactly what I needed. But over time, maintaining the site started to feel heavier than writing on it. For a personal blog, that is usually a sign that something needs to change.

I wanted this site to feel lighter again, both technically and mentally.

## Why I migrated (top 3 reasons)

### 1) Modernization

This one is simple: I wanted a setup that feels current.

Astro’s content-first, static-first model fits how I use this site today. Most of my blog is content, not app-like interactivity. Astro makes that path feel natural.

### 2) More control

I wanted to control the structure and presentation directly without fighting too much framework abstraction.

With Astro, I can keep things simple, shape components the way I want, and evolve page by page. For a long-lived personal site, this kind of control matters a lot.

### 3) Faster design iteration (with Claude)

The migration design itself was done with AI-assisted iteration. I used Claude while shaping the new design and structure.

It was useful for quickly exploring layout and style directions, then refining manually. I’ll write more about that process in a separate post.

## Gatsby got old for my use case

This is not a complaint post about Gatsby. It’s more that my needs changed.

I now prefer a setup with less moving parts for a personal publishing workflow. Astro feels like a better long-term fit for where this site is headed.

## What is still pending

The previous blog had a commenting system, and I still need to modernize that for this Astro version. That is one of the next updates.
